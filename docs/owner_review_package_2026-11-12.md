# HAS review copy

Open a project with its **Open … .bat** launcher. **Start review.bat** opens Home.
This separate copy keeps its own preferences and recovery; the installed Studio
and earlier reviews remain available. Keep the `Studio` folder beside the projects
and launchers.

| Launcher | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| Open Avatar | The supplied VRM avatar for posing, camera movement and imported hair |
| Open Flatgrass | The installed Source map with its supported materials and baked lighting |
| Open Street | The supplied MMD environment and its original atlas textures |
| Open Reimu helpers | The earlier configured ribbon example with a fan |
| Open Back hair demo | A test-created ribbon plus native Back hair setup and a fan; this is a synthetic demo, not your recorded take |
| Open Mima head expressions | Mima's head, hair and hat with 143 prepared expression targets; this is head-only, not a complete character |

**Scene setup** fills the available 3D area in Scene / camera and Pose model.
**Camera focus** returns to the output-shaped view. **Frame** and **3×3** are
optional composition guides and stay out of renders. Draw keeps its paper zoom
and pan. World, Properties and Timeline can be resized, split, tabbed or floated
using their dock controls.

The pale outer rotation ring turns relative to the camera; RGB rings follow their
axes. Hold **Ctrl** during camera flight to slow down, then release it to restore
ordinary speed.

In loose-part setup, click a visible part or use the list to correct the guess.
The orange outline marks the focused part. **Include in setup** keeps a reviewed
plan while another part is focused; the final button applies all checked plans
as one edit. Shared native-joint areas are linked and disclosed. Switching
**Hair & clothes** off removes live secondary offsets while preserving authored
poses and explicitly recorded motion. Local-blow preview is still separate
development work in this copy.

For Mima, select the head and open **Shapes / expressions…**. Its facial targets
are separate from Source skins and bodygroup controls. Skin/bodygroup switching,
native jiggle matching and joining separate character components remain unfinished.

Output defaults to **1920 × 1080 at 24 fps**. This copy also has 720p and 540p
delivery presets; 4K/custom dimensions and changing resolution on the timeline
are unfinished. Higher-quality lighting presets and RTX-like lighting remain
future work.

This folder uses this PC's existing **Python, Godot 4.6 and FFmpeg**. Adding new
native files also uses the existing conversion tools; the launchers point to the
approved SourceIO snapshot `fe24260b97ac03b899d9b5d4f0fd7b34772f3647` in this
session's dependencies folder. No tool or installed Studio was replaced.

All six projects passed automated startup, loading, saved-state and render-replay
checks. Those checks do not certify original-model appearance, novice/learner
experience, physical tablet/audio behavior or a clean installation. Exact source
details are in `source-snapshot-verification.json`.

Keep this private review while it contains work, preferences or recovery without
a backup. Its model/project artwork is not uploaded to the repository.
