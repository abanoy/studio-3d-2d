**Scene setup** fills the available 3D area. **Camera focus** shows the output framing. **Frame** and **3×3** are optional guides. Draw retains its paper zoom and pan. World, Properties and Timeline can be resized, split, tabbed or floated.

The pale outer rotation ring turns relative to the camera; RGB rings follow their axes. Hold **Ctrl** during camera flight to slow down, then release it for ordinary speed. Releasing camera control returns the pointer to where the gesture began.

In **Set up loose parts**, click a visible area or correct the guess in the list. The orange outline shows the focused part. **Include in setup** keeps several reviewed parts for one Apply. **Try a local blow** now tries the reviewed setup before Apply. After preparation, separate wind-group checks and strength/direction controls appear. Clicking a wind row changes focus; its check controls only this temporary try-out. Apply inclusion remains separate. Linked groups are disclosed because shared weights can move several areas together.

Stop the try-out or press Escape to restore the original view. Save, Record, rendering, closing or editing the reviewed setup also stop it. Apply remains an explicit edit with Undo. To review an already moving model, first turn its Physics and Hair & clothes off. Independent scene peers can continue during the try-out; pause them before Apply.

For already configured chains, **Review local wind…** remains available. Turning **Hair & clothes** off removes live secondary offsets and preserves authored poses and explicitly recorded motion.

For Mima, select the head and open **Shapes / expressions…**. Facial targets are separate from Source skins/bodygroups. Full character assembly, skin/bodygroup switching and matching native jiggle behavior remain unfinished.

Output defaults to **1920 × 1080 at 24 fps**, with 720p and 540p delivery presets. 4K/custom dimensions, timeline resolution changes, future lighting modes and online Discovery remain roadmap work.
